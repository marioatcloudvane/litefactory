# Research serialization

Record request plus actual findings, decisions and assumptions with stable IDs.
Completed describes finished investigation; incomplete preserves useful findings and
what remains; blocked explains the obstruction. Do not manufacture implementation.
Questions may be included only with trusted permission on incomplete/blocked exits.

Executable application-v1 examples (synthetic offline identities):
- [completed](examples/research-completed.json)
- [incomplete](examples/research-incomplete.json)
- [blocked](examples/research-blocked.json)
- [revision-checked update](examples/research-update.json)
- [permitted questioning shape](examples/research-question.json); Plan uses its own payload.
