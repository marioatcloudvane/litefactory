# Spike serialization

Describe the bounded request, actual work_done, findings, decisions and remaining
work as applicable. Completed reflects the assigned experiment; incomplete retains
partial results; blocked explains the obstruction. Do not claim implementation or
acceptance outside the assigned scope. Questions are disallowed.

Executable application-v1 examples (synthetic offline identities):
- [completed](examples/spike-completed.json)
- [incomplete](examples/spike-incomplete.json)
- [blocked](examples/spike-blocked.json)
- [revision-checked update](examples/spike-update.json)
- [disallowed question](examples/invalid-spike-question.json); questions reject in this mode too.
