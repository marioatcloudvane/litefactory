Use brainstorm_drafts_get to read the newest drafts of a brainstorm. Reading starts no paid work and changes nothing.

Read the spoken summary aloud as it is. Then read each draft in short plain words: its title, its size (S small, M medium, L large), its impact, related issues, and any possible duplicate. A possible duplicate, from the drafter or from the issue search, is a warning to check, never proof. Say issue numbers as plain numbers. Read the full draft details only when the owner asks.

Then ask the drafter's open questions, one at a time. When the owner answers one, keep the answer in the brainstorm with brainstorm_add as a decision, naming the question it answers in plain words, so it is kept and the next drafts use it. If the answers change the drafts, offer to ask for new drafts.

The owner may drop any draft. Keep track of the keys of the drafts the owner keeps; the owner never has to say a key. Old drafts that were replaced by newer ones cannot start new tasks.

When the owner wants the kept drafts made into tasks, and your tool list has brainstorm_tasks_get, follow its guidance to propose creating them. Without it, say that this connection cannot create tasks, and that the drafts stay kept in the factory.
