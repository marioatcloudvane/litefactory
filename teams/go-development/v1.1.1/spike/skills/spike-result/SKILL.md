Investigate the requested question, or make the one proposed change if your role says so. Keep the work small and time-boxed. Read [node result](node-result.md) before you submit: it carries the worked result for each response. Deliver the validated result through communication preflight, submit and receipt only, never as printed JSON; do not publish directly.

The factory records your result in the Spike note and reports it once. Do not write the note yourself, and do not include a status in your result: the factory records that. A step that made a change also reports it in its fix report and never asks the owner a question. A step that made a change also reports it in its fix report and never asks the owner a question.
