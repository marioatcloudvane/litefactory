# T-100.2 — Durable schedules, prerequisites, and proposals

> As an owner, I want queued work and proposals to survive restarts so that accepted work is neither lost nor duplicated.

This story provides durable state and atomic lifecycle operations for scheduling.

## Why This Matters

A future job cannot live only in memory: a restart or repeated poll could otherwise lose it or start it twice. Dependencies and proposal acceptance need equally reliable records.

## Architecture Notes

- Add one embedded migration and context-first store accessors for schedules, prerequisite edges, proposals, and proposal items.
- Use source-comment idempotency, deterministic eligible ordering, conditional pending-to-running reservation, and atomic acceptance.
- Keep all SQL/transactions in `internal/store` and retain one SQLite connection.
