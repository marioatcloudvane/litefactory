# Owner-selected inventory source

Owner guidance for this continuation selects the final Research comment below together with story-002 AC-005/T-003's explicit runtime-family assignment. Unlisted branches are out of scope. This resolves the previous missing-source blocker without inventing a hidden branch list.

Source: https://github.com/marioatcloudvane/litefactory/issues/1067#issuecomment-6395840226

The proposed scope is one owner-guidance standard across every owner-facing stop, park, refusal, wait and redo. An owner must be able to see why work stopped, what it holds up, what still runs, and the exact permitted action to take next. The source audit now includes the remaining Doctor, conversation, transport and lifecycle surfaces; the retained inventory distinguishes deficient, locally compliant and inapplicable branches.

## In scope

- Cover factory reports and agent instructions across Research, Plan, Implement, Validate, Bug, Spike, Doctor and conversation, including configuration, admission, permissions, scheduling, stale inputs, budgets, failures, delivery and owner-requested stops.
- Begin full stop reports with a plain-language cause in one or two sentences, then identify affected work, continuing work and ordered next actions.
- Give each available option its exact supported command or reply, where to send it, who may use it, any text or evidence the owner must supply, and what happens afterward.
- Recommend one option when a real choice exists, offer only supported alternatives, and say when waiting is the correct action or an operator must intervene.
- Derive guidance from existing retained cause, task, story, dependency, finding, execution, revision, permission and effect state, marking unknown facts explicitly.
- Use existing command and reply channels, including current displayed decision references and explicit revisions, with fresh guidance after a target, head or evidence changes.
- Show parked stories together with the waiting work they hold up and give decision-specific guidance that reaches the correct story.
- Order prerequisites before other shipping blockers and optional decisions, while stating which actions are independent.
- Explain Validate pass, fail, accept, defer, repair, follow-up issue, return and delivery consequences separately wherever permitted.
- Preserve the existing reasoned shipping waiver, explaining which displayed blockers it waives and that factual test and review evidence remains unchanged.
- Preserve the distinction between continuation of the same graph, which keeps its limits, and supported story or failed-coordinator restart paths, which can start fresh limits.
- Preserve each role's existing authority, including factory-published resolver questions and the prohibition on direct questions from story workers.
- Keep every recovery item reachable when reports are shortened, with a real linked destination containing its full commands and consequences.
- Let short chat notices link full guidance while still stating the cause, affected work and immediate next step.
- Explain partial and uncertain remote effects before offering retry, and identify the supported inspection route when repeating an action could duplicate it.
- Retain stops with safe fallback guidance when the reason is missing or unusable, without inventing causes or promising that another reply fixes the problem.
- Give authorized operators precise restoration or inspection steps when no owner command can unblock work, while preserving access checks and protected information.
- Update maintained Go, .NET, Python and Swift instruction sources and shared agents through new supported releases, with an explicit upgrade boundary for installed teams.
- Verify the four guidance requirements on full reports, including reports assembled from several locally compliant pieces.

Acceptance requires the classified source inventory to remain complete for the changed reporting surfaces, generated commands to satisfy existing grammar and state gates, and checks covering dependent waits, stale decisions, long reports, permission refusals, both restart paths, shipping waivers, partial effects and unavailable delivery.

## Out of scope

- New workflow commands, approval powers, retry or restart semantics, scheduling policies or automatic recovery behavior.
- Database redesign, a new dashboard, successful progress prose that requests no action, or internal-only errors.
- Bypassing channel authorization or creating a new notification route when publication is unavailable.
- Rewriting historical releases, changing captured sessions or silently upgrading installed teams.
- Taking over issue #1128's shared reporting-skill design or its dependency; its agent-written explanations overlap, while factory stops remain here.
- Claiming live reproduction of the original incident or verification of every installed release and provider environment.

The audit establishes current behavior; the guidance contract, linked chat format and release boundary are shaping decisions for your review. Existing durable records remain authoritative, so this scope requires no new stored entity.
