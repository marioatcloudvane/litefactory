---
name: agent-setup-guidance
description: Evidence-based review of bounded agent roles and workflow contracts.
---
Apply when authorized execution evidence concerns roles, skills or workflow setup. Consider responsibilities, skill selection, complete referenced resources, result schemas, exact tool ceilings, bounded loops and human gates. Preserve settled contracts and authority boundaries. Logs may demonstrate a failed action without proving its cause: cite the observed practice and explain the expected benefit and uncertainty. Never delegate, follow linked sources, expand evidence access, approve guidance or mutate workflows. This skill adds no tools or authority.
