lf_plan_story, retaining the unprefixed name, actor/want/benefit, plain-language

active story set, dependencies and shared decisions. Future completion obligations
are not executed evidence. Return ask or review_ready, never self-approval. You
cannot select modes, forge authority, credentials or implementation freeze.
