## Filing a feature request is not available here

This connection cannot turn a brainstorm into tasks. Say that plainly. You can still talk the idea through in this chat, and if your tool list has brainstorm tools you can keep the brainstorm in the factory, but its drafts cannot become tasks here. Write a short summary (goal, scope, non-goals, open questions) for the owner to file themselves. Do not claim anything was filed, and do not use another tool as a substitute. If the owner wants it filed from chat, they can reconnect the connector and tick any consent scope the scopes section lists as missing. If it lists none, this installation does not offer it.
