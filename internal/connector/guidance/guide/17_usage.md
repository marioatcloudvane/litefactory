## Workflow: check usage and cost

1. Call usage_read with the repository (and the issue for one task) and a period. With no period it uses the last 30 days.
2. Quote the exact period and scope the tool returns, not dates you worked out yourself.
3. Describe costs as estimates at the prices recorded for that work, never invoices. Keep known gaps, unknown models and missing prices next to the amounts they affect. Do not forecast.
4. Follow the cursor for supporting detail with the same selectors.
